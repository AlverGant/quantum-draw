    How it works

    Four steps, all of them checkable by a stranger.

    01

    Quantum entropy, harvested ahead

    We put qubits on IBM hardware into superposition and measure them. The results are split into pulses and sealed under a Merkle root published before any draw exists.

    02

    Your list gets committed

    When you create a draw we hash the list, the winner count and the exact future pulse it will use. Changing anything afterwards breaks the hash.

    03

    A beacon nobody controls

    The seed also mixes in a future drand round. It does not exist yet when you commit, so not even we can pick a pulse that suits us.

    04

    Anyone can recompute it

    Given the proof, the shuffle is deterministic. Our verifier runs in your browser and fetches the beacon straight from drand — never through our servers.

    The Bell test

    Every pool ships a CHSH test run in the same job that harvested the entropy. Well-spread numbers prove nothing on their own — a classical generator makes those too. This is the measurement a classical generator cannot fake.
